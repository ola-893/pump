# pump

The project and platform brand is **pump**, written in lowercase.

**pump coin** is the token investors receive from a strategy vault. Each
investable strategy version has its own vault and pump coin. Use **pump** for
the platform and **pump coin** for the token, including wallet labels, balances,
receipts and new NEAR token metadata.

Historical evidence schemas, request headers, persisted storage keys, signed
message domains, deployment account IDs and old contract identifiers retain
their original names. Renaming those would change compatibility or historical
evidence. Use the current brand in customer-facing copy.

## Landing-page message

Primary tagline: **Privacy-first trading models.**

Lead with local-only inference: a trading strategy is the operator’s intellectual
property, and strategy prompts are not sent to hosted LLM vendors.

Secondary tagline: **Inspectable agents. Transparent records for investors.**

Keep this promise specific to model inference. The current registration has a
public strategy description, and evidence exports may include prompts. Do not
claim complete strategy secrecy, confidential exports or hardware attestation.
